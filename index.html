Purpose

  • Share the vision and expectations with all club and cell coordinators (CCMs) and get well-structured, standardised event proposals from each club and cell by 15.10.2026.

Vision

  • Value-driven experiences: attractive prize money and sessions with thought leaders.
  • Management events: draw participation from leading Tier 1 and Tier 2 B-schools in major cities.
  • Cultural events: draw participation from Tier 3 colleges to broaden outreach and inclusivity.
  • Keep competition rounds concise and engaging.
  • Invite prominent, relevant judges for individual events.
  • Secure event-specific sponsorships suited to each event.

Benchmarking and theme

  • Set event standards by studying management and cultural fests of Tier 1 B-schools.
  • Theme to be announced on 05.10.2026.

  • CCMs asked for a full outreach plan and a clear rules and regulations guide for framing events.
  • Each event gets two senior CCMs and two junior CCMs.

Concerns raised by Symphonies

  • Availability of a good-quality sound system.
  • Attractive prize money to encourage participation.
  • Delivering a worthwhile, memorable experience.
  • Permission to extend activities until 12:00 a.m.

Accommodation, food and operations

  • Explore tie-ups with paying guest accommodations and residences near the college.
  • Let participants buy food coupons at registration and choose their food in advance.
  • Plan guest and judge coordination, hospitality and food, participant assembly areas, and seating before and after events.

Suggestions

  • Explore non-vegetarian food stalls outside campus, subject to institutional permission.
  • Make cultural events more attractive so management-event participants also join them.

Action points

  • All clubs and cells submit event lists by 15.10.2026.
  • Finalise and announce the theme on 05.10.2026.
  • Prepare an outreach plan and a participant rules and regulations guide.
  • Identify accommodation and food arrangements.
  • Plan sound, hospitality, seating and participant management.
  • Explore event-specific sponsorships and ways to improve engagement.
