CCM briefing on vision and events

02 Oct 2026

The file header reads 02-10-2023. Treated as 02-10-2026 because the minutes refer to YUKTI '27 and 2026 deadlines.

Purpose

  • Share the vision and expectations with all club and cell coordinators (CCMs) and get well-structured, standardised event proposals from each club and cell by 15.10.2026.

Vision

  • Value-driven experiences: attractive prize money and sessions with thought leaders.
  • Management events: draw participation from leading Tier 1 and Tier 2 B-schools in major cities.
  • Cultural events: draw participation from Tier 3 colleges to broaden outreach and inclusivity.
  • Keep competition rounds concise and engaging.
  • Invite prominent, relevant judges for individual events.
  • Secure event-specific sponsorships suited to each event.

Benchmarking and theme

  • Set event standards by studying management and cultural fests of Tier 1 B-schools.
  • Theme to be announced on 05.10.2026.

Event planning and coordination

  • CCMs asked for a full outreach plan and a clear rules and regulations guide for framing events.
  • Each event gets two senior CCMs and two junior CCMs.
  • A smooth communication channel between CCMs across clubs and cells is a key requirement.

Concerns raised by Symphonies

  • Availability of a good-quality sound system.
  • Attractive prize money to encourage participation.
  • Delivering a worthwhile, memorable experience.
  • Permission to extend activities until 12:00 a.m.

Accommodation, food and operations

  • Explore tie-ups with paying guest accommodations and residences near the college.
  • Let participants buy food coupons at registration and choose their food in advance.
  • Plan guest and judge coordination, hospitality and food, participant assembly areas, and seating before and after events.

Suggestions

  • Explore non-vegetarian food stalls outside campus, subject to institutional permission.
  • Make cultural events more attractive so management-event participants also join them.

Action points

  • All clubs and cells submit event lists by 15.10.2026.
  • Finalise and announce the theme on 05.10.2026.
  • Prepare an outreach plan and a participant rules and regulations guide.
  • Identify accommodation and food arrangements.
  • Plan sound, hospitality, seating and participant management.
  • Explore event-specific sponsorships and ways to improve engagement.

Meeting with Manjula ma'am

07 Oct 2026

Event design

  • Keep about 15 professional events, with a little something for everyone who comes.
  • Mix online and offline events, and mix every domain; pull in the Sports club.
  • Quality is based on the number of rounds, so keep many rounds.
  • Individual events (for example Best Manager): as many rounds as possible, stretched across all 3 days.
  • Team events pull revenue; keep spot registration open for them.
  • Have treasure hunt and simulation ideas; consider running them virtually.
  • Live LED scoreboard to keep up the excitement.
  • Culturals after 5 p.m.: 4 hours a day, 12 hours over 3 days.

People and roles

  • Every event has a faculty coordinator, lead coordinators (2nd year) and a co-coordinator (1st year).
  • Appoint a stage manager to keep stage and audience engaged; prepare a stage agenda; TSM students present at each event.
  • Keep event coordinators and the team in the loop; schedule frequent meetings.
  • Connect with alumni, including those over 10 years out, as jury. Grow from 36 jury members to about 100.

Budget

  • Think about reimbursements.
  • Draft a line budget first: stage ₹5L, management events ₹2.5L, entertainment ₹2.5L, cash rewards, travel reimbursement (other-state students, jury).
  • Fix the budget, then move to sponsorship.

Sponsorship

  • Tap PROs and CSR initiative heads.
  • Approach Muthoot Finance, Equitas, HDFC and TMB.
  • Tap SaaS, payment gateway, edtech and AI platform companies, anyone who wants to reach the student community.
  • Classify sponsors as overall, event-based and award-based.
  • Form separate teams for edtech, food and corporates.
  • Pitch to hospitals.
  • Brand TSM.

Celebrities and promotion

  • Names suggested: Aravindh Swamy, Sidd Ahmed, Madhavan, Vikram, Mithul Jai Shankar, Sasi Kumar, Shivaangi, Krish and Sangeetha, Rishikanth.
  • Look into FM advertisements.
  • Aim for YUKTI '27 to be bigger than the past three years.

Stalls, brochures, stay and timing

  • Many small stalls rather than one stall with many varieties; lac bangle and other trending stalls.
  • Two separate brochures, one for management and one for culturals.
  • Speak with the mandapams at Thiruparankundram about accommodation.
  • Plan to hold the next YUKTI in September or October.
